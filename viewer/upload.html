SLAM It!
visual odometry on your own video

Run it on your own video

Upload a phone video and the C++ pipeline tracks the camera through it. You get a link to the run viewer: every frame as the pipeline saw it, its keypoints, and the map it built.

What are you uploading?

On a phone, this offers to record a video now, or to choose one you have.

What to expect

  • Up to 60 seconds and 500 MB, uploaded within 15 minutes: 500 MB needs about 4.5 Mbit/s.
  • A video runs monocular: one camera cannot tell size, so the scale is unknown, and there is no ground truth to compare with.
  • A Stray Scanner recording runs RGB-D with its LiDAR depth, at true scale, and is compared with ARKit's poses.
  • Stabilisation crops and warps every frame, so the camera stops behaving like one fixed lens. Turn it off where the phone allows; apps such as Blackmagic Camera can.
  • Getting started: move sideways at first, not only turning. Texture and good light help. A lost track ends the run, and the viewer shows where and why.
  • Rolling shutter is ignored. Moving slowly keeps its effect small.

What is kept, and who can see it

  • The result (the frames as the pipeline saw them, the camera's path and the map) is kept for 7 days, then deleted.
  • Anyone with the result's link can see it. There is no gallery or listing, and search engines are asked to stay away.
  • The server logs what happens to each run by its number, never by its link. The service it runs on logs requests, with the addresses they come from.
  • The pages load their fonts from Google, and the viewer its 3D library from jsDelivr, which see your address as any site you load from does.
